The idea

Two languages for one mystery

Physics describes reality in equations. The rishis described it in mantras, stories and silence. Quant-aum puts them side by side — not to prove that one predicted the other, but to see where they rhyme, where they differ, and what each can teach the other.

Quantum Latin · "how much"

The discovery that energy comes in discrete packets — and that at its smallest scale, reality behaves in ways that challenge common sense.

Aum Sanskrit · ॐ

The primordial sound of the Upanishads — said to contain all that was, is and will be, and the silence beyond it.
