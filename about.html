About

A learner standing in two worlds

Quant-aum explains, simply and honestly, how modern science and India's spiritual wisdom illuminate each other.

What is Quant-aum?

Quant comes from quantum, the smallest packet of energy and the door into modern physics. Aum (ॐ) is the primordial sound of the Upanishads, the symbol of all that exists and the awareness beyond it. Quant-aum is the space where the two meet.

Here you'll find essays, and soon podcast episodes, reels and videos, that take a story or teaching from the scriptures, especially Advaita Vedanta, and read it alongside what science has discovered. Sometimes the two rhyme beautifully. Sometimes they don't, and that's worth saying too.

How I write

  • Simple first. If an idea can't be explained to a curious teenager, I haven't understood it yet.
  • Honest labels. I'll always make clear what is established science, what a scripture says, and what is my own analogy.
  • Resonance, not proof. I don't claim the ancient texts "predicted" modern physics, or that physics "proves" spirituality. I look for shared patterns and shared questions.
  • Stories matter. The Puranas carry ideas in stories. I take them seriously as carriers of meaning.

Who's writing

I'm Sudheer Devalla, based in Hyderabad. I studied computer science, built a career in technology and business, and have always been drawn to the stories and philosophy of the Indian tradition. I'm not a physicist or a monk. I'm a student of both worlds, learning in public, and Quant-aum is my notebook.

If something here sparks a thought, or you spot a mistake, I'd love to hear from you. A contact form is coming soon.
